# PED-07: ordinary fall, recovery and volume ownership

## Production changes

The normal original population retains the PED-06 bounded100-process and
85-ground-record owners. PED-07 completes their fall/recovery continuations;
it adds no second movement, corpse launch or speed-based sleep timer.

- Ordinary active halt supplies NULL for its optional normal, matching installed
  HW `4d0de0`. The halt kernel selects the first maximum positive-Y normal from
  the actual retained physical faces. A first wall normal is not the floor.
- Source direction selection treats an existing process with NULL selected
  support as a complete no-op (`4cc06e..4cc07f`). Missing canonical ownership
  remains an error. Proximity also respects the source NULL early return.
- Living recovery stages the forced source recache/direction/proximity pass
  before halt mutations or CRT draws. Transient process bytes and copied ordered
  faces validate the actual post-halt action (upright1, lying4). The preview may
  publish derived geometry caches; it cannot pull ACTs into PHIL or stop a
  GROOVE. Required gameplay mutations remain explicit providers.
- Native simple and detached-bone bodies use their existing canonical quiet
  providers. They are excluded from generic hierarchy retention: their `owner`
  field previously enrolled a second quiet state, evaluated the same body twice
  and hid a real quiet3 leaf behind generic quiet0. Complete mixed contact rings
  still govern sleep; grounded cars still force their source hierarchy awake.
- Successful source vanish releases current simple/bone PHIL and render owners
  without halt, Kill, BON advance or random draws. The release can remain pending
  until actual headers disappear. Only then does normal population accounting
  commit once. Vanish supplies flag80; OneLess supplies100. HP is unchanged and
  vanish does not award a death or fabricate blood/transport.
- Selected-volume Munge, immersed activation and moved continuations use the
  actual process/body/shared CRT. See [volume evidence](c2-ped-ordinary-volume-re.md).
  The30-second living timeout calls the existing full Kill owner, including its
  count-before-score effects, without an invented immortal check or forced113.

## Source boundaries

The installed proximity scanner admits a raw negative MAT group at
`4cff35..4cff51`, then can index prepared groups unchecked at
`4d0534..4d053e`. That is an original out-of-bounds path, not permission to
substitute group0. The browser reports
`source-proximity-unchecked-negative-prepared-group-storage` before process,
face, root, move or CRT changes. Existing exact scanner captures remain valid.

Fresh passive physical initializers have no selected volume. Passive preparation
does not select one. Activation resets the automatic candidate/depth through
the existing reset helper, while actual selection remains in the active force
phase. A previously active body may retain a selected volume. Re-querying every
passive Munge would change source chronology and is deliberately avoided.

Retained joint2/full articulated FORM ownership belongs to PED-08. Complete
award/effect adapters belong to PED-09. Replay recording remains explicitly off.
Negative napalm-resource lookup and the native all-zero random omega singularity
retain the documented explicit boundaries in the volume note.
