# Compatibility

## Executive Summary

The engine loads stock and Advanced tracks and cars from a local installation, including original geometry, materials, pixelmaps, and TIFF textures. Driving, crashes, water, oil, lifts, world destruction, noncars and mines have source-based runtime paths, stock/headless tests and Chromium validation. Mission, campaign, AI, and several animation systems remain incomplete; [Backlog](../backlog/README.md) tracks priorities and remaining gaps.

## Supported content

The asset service reads loose files and Carmageddon TWT archives. Readers cover BRender DAT geometry, ACT hierarchies, MAT materials, PIX/P16 pixelmaps, TIFF textures, WAM deformation metadata, BON/SKL motion data, and track/car/race configuration. Track setup uses source grid positions, gates, race paths, sky settings, and available lap and timer values. Car setup reads practical mechanical values and model references. Missing pixelmap names can fall back to filenames.

The browser reuses original scene and vehicle assets. Its menus and HUD are new browser UI. Opponents follow available path graphs. WAM crush planes, part softness, bend/split fields and damage programs drive localized permanent deformation, twelve component levels and physical detachable parts. Component faults affect the drivetrain, steering, brakes and wheels. Ordinary recovery restores source-safe position/rotation history instead of a nearest-path projection. Damage-triggered WASTED uses original SCROLLERS text, font archives, timing, rival queue and the GENERAL-configured explosion WAV. Positional audio outlets, rival AI and some race rules remain incomplete.

Pedestrians use full original surface-density populations (New City1 791, Airport1 573, Carrier1 249), personality/movement data and native camera processing. Recovered threat prediction, panic/run callbacks, smooth turns and finite-face avoidance govern behavior. BON/SKL root displacement supplies locomotion averages; full skeletal/root-frame playback is still incomplete and limbs animate procedurally. Original cloud textures, chunk models, persistent slicks and smears use source effect quantities/timing. Their collision-force input and corpse/contact motion remain estimated until native pedestrian collision reconstruction (PED-03). See [pedestrian evidence](c2-pedestrians-re.md). Engine, impact, GENERAL-configured world pickup, checkpoint, final-lap, completion, out-of-time, original wasted and gesture-unlock sounds are supported. See [event audio evidence](c2-event-audio-re.md) for routing and remaining spatial/effect limits.

Partial funk/groove behavior includes rolling and steering objects, brake-light atlas changes, water, and fans. World GROOVE poses preserve source actor hierarchy; 212 stock blocks parse. Relative-frame contact against finite moving triangles is implemented independently, but exact native platform callbacks and original stop-animation behavior remain open. Four-wheel suspension, steering, tyre limits, gears and angular dynamics use the independently reconstructed solver. Race completion supports configured laps, eliminating all opponents, or eliminating pedestrians when selected; Smash and Smash-and-Ped preserve authored targets and use installed signed-byte mission counters updated by real world connotations and selected pedestrian deaths. Race exclusions substitute definition 0; no-time flags suppress ordinary time awards. Award quantities, population-dependent goals and script-dependent mission traversal still have gaps; see [race rules evidence](c2-race-rules-re.md).

Power-up support covers original placed credit, repair, time bonuses/freezing, weighted random effects, engine/grip, invulnerability, free repairs, damage magnification, granite protection, APO levels/slots and active-effect cancellation. Actions 34/35/36 provide cumulative negative-`initialValue` charge inventories, selected with Tab and activated with F; command jump adds the authored world-Y impulse, recovery vouchers bypass charges only after successful recovery, and timed instant handbrake zeros linear velocity/RPM without ordinary rear braking. Mine, mortar, oil-drop, direct mine-hit and visual crush paths are integrated. Mine mass comes from GENERAL, and explosion animation uses the source frame setting. Mine sphere contacts currently use a sampled convex approximation (PHY-05); no radial explosion damage is applied. OILDRUM definition data includes three stock actors above the original 32-polyvertex limit, which produces a warning and static fallback. Remaining unsupported effects are collected with an explicit `NOT IMPLEMENTED` notification.

## Known limits

Source water/oil formulas and native noncar lift activation, forces, joints and finite travel have stock tests and browser checks. GROOVE-driven geometry uses an independent relative-frame contact integration; exact native interruption callbacks remain unproven. Mine sphere contacts remain sampled. Native noncar wire points/edges are supported; custom-car wire integration remains open. World smash state, material changes, removal/replacement, sound, explosion animation and delayed activation run; Variable connotations now update the native-width mission memory; shrapnel/ghostparts, awards and scripts remain incomplete. FLIC playback, replays, campaign progression/unlocks, remaining power-up effects, full mission logic, and BON/SKL playback remain incomplete.

Some source content has concrete data issues. Legacy ACT string references with stale chunk lengths, including `CPOINT.ACT`, are supported. `PROG.ACT` lacks a BRender header. The ADV Digga ACT is truncated; two Forklift DATs contain non-finite UV values, normalized with diagnostics while preserving strict finite geometry parsing. All 52 stock car mechanics/shape pipelines now load, including Forklift. The Nuke race config has no player start and its expected ACT is absent. These entries may fail to load or cannot be driven as configured. Some mission/arena configs contain no usable checkpoint gates and therefore cannot complete a checkpoint race.

The Advanced installation also contains malformed MAT and UI PIX files. Some Advanced track texture references point to TIFF images in nested directories; those are supported, but unresolvable or missing source files can still produce fallback materials or incomplete surfaces. Large scenes may be limited by browser and GPU memory, especially when building collision queries.

## Tests

`npm test` runs unit and fixture tests; tests requiring the original installation skip when it is absent. Coverage includes native wheel-ray tolerances, Eagle/Monster contour transitions and source recovery history, three full pedestrian populations, threat/avoidance updates, blood effects and original WASTED fonts/queues. All 212 valid stock noncar definitions construct complete bodies, including five repaired monorail instances and two authored wire forms; three oversized OILDRUM definitions remain intentional parse failures. `npm run test:browser` builds the app and launches installed Chromium through Playwright Core. Checks cover stock/Advanced driving/races, mine contact damage/deformation/detachment/repair, oil, finite water, Carrier lift, Airport localized door destruction, real-key Snow Way Out recovery, rival-triggered pedestrian panic, blood and WASTED feedback. Chrome DevTools MCP separately verifies actual world-prop collisions, lift travel/end stop, rotating GROOVEs, original WASTED glyphs/WAV decoding, blood rendering and console state. Screenshots are stored in ignored test artifacts. These checks use software WebGL and do not establish hardware-GPU performance or full-game parity.
